Privacy Policy

How this independently operated personal injury information and lead-generation website handles information associated with visits and telephone inquiries.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the information that may be collected, used, and shared when you visit personal-injury-lawyer-phoenix.com (the “Website”) or call the phone number displayed on it. The Website is an independent informational and lead-generation website, not a law firm or a direct provider of legal services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Website and whether you make a telephone inquiry, information collected or received may include:

  • Information you provide voluntarily, such as your name, contact details, and description of your inquiry.
  • Telephone and caller information, such as your phone number, caller identification details, the time and duration of a call, and information you share while speaking with a recipient of your inquiry.
  • Inquiry details, including the type of accident or legal assistance you are seeking, if you choose to discuss them.
  • Basic technical information, such as IP address, browser or device information, and server request information that a website host may process as part of operating the Website.

The current homepage does not contain a visitor-submission form. This policy does not assume that forms, analytics tools, or advertising trackers are in use.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, help connect callers with relevant independent service providers, facilitate services or consultations you request, assess the quality and operation of the Website and its inquiries, prevent fraud or misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Lead Generation and Third-Party Service Providers

The Website is designed to generate telephone inquiries about personal injury legal assistance. If you call the displayed number, your inquiry may be routed or referred to an independent third-party business or professional who may be able to discuss the services you are seeking. The Website operator may receive compensation for qualifying inquiries or referrals.

Those third parties operate independently, determine which services they offer, and are responsible for their own services, communications, and privacy practices. A call does not create an attorney-client relationship with the Website operator, and calling does not by itself authorize unrelated marketing calls or messages.

5. Call Tracking and Recording

Telephone inquiries may be routed or tracked through third-party telecommunications services, which may process call routing details, caller identification, timestamps, and call duration. Whether a particular call is recorded has not been confirmed for this Website. If recording is used, appropriate notice and consent must be provided where required by applicable law. You should not assume all calls are recorded.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Cookies are small files that some websites place on a visitor's device, and analytics tools can be used to understand how a website is used. The supplied Website files do not contain a configured analytics platform, advertising pixel, or cookie-consent feature. We do not claim that any particular analytics or advertising service is active. Hosting providers may separately generate routine technical logs needed to deliver the Website.

7. Information Sharing

Information associated with your inquiry may be shared with relevant independent service providers to help respond to your request. Information may also be processed by technical service providers involved in hosting the Website or handling telephone calls. Information may be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent misuse and protect legal rights. The Website is not represented as a service that keeps all inquiry information exclusively within its own systems.

8. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect information handled in connection with the Website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information should be retained only as needed for inquiries, operational purposes, and applicable legal requirements. A specific retention period has not been established or verified for this Website; third-party providers may have their own retention policies.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the laws that apply to you and the information held, you may be entitled to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You can also choose not to provide optional information during an inquiry. If you are connected with an independent provider, direct requests about information held by that provider to the provider itself.

A verified privacy-request contact method for the Website operator has not yet been supplied. The operator should publish a working contact method before the page goes live so visitors can raise privacy questions or exercise any applicable rights.

10. Third-Party Websites and Services

Independent service providers, telephone service companies, and external websites may have different privacy policies and practices. Review their policies before providing information directly to them. The Website operator does not control their independent services.

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Website, its referral arrangements, or its information-handling practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
